# Chapter 4 — Why Should We Learn Kubernetes

## Objective
Explain the real-world reason why Kubernetes is an important skill for a DevOps Engineer role, and connect it to the shift from Monolithic to Microservices applications (explained fully in Chapter 5).

## Key Teaching Points

1. Among all the roles a DevOps Engineer performs, **the demand for Kubernetes skills is very high** in the industry.
2. The reason for this high demand: many companies used to build applications as a single big block (**Monolithic** applications). Now, most companies have shifted to building smaller, independent pieces (**Microservices**).
3. Managing many small microservices manually is hard — this is exactly the problem Kubernetes solves.

## Classroom Note
This is a short, motivational/context chapter. It directly leads into Chapter 5 (Monolithic vs Microservices), where the actual reasoning is explained in detail with an example. Keep this chapter brief in class — just 1-2 lines — and move to Chapter 5 for the full explanation.

## Final Result
Students understand that Kubernetes skill demand exists **because of the industry shift toward microservices**, which sets up the next topic.
