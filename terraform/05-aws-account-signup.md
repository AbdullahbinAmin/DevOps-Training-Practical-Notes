# Chapter 5 — AWS Account Sign Up

## Objective
Create a personal AWS account to use for learning.

## Prerequisites
- A real email address (fake emails do not work because AWS verifies it)
- A debit or credit card (international payments enabled)
- A phone number that can receive an SMS or call
- An ID document number (in the video: driving licence)

## Official page
**https://aws.amazon.com** → click **Create an AWS Account**.

⚠️ Verification Required: AWS changes the sign-up screens and the free tier rules from time to time (new accounts may get a credit-based free plan). Read the plan shown on your screen before you accept it.

## GUI Steps

**Step 1:** Open https://aws.amazon.com and click **Create an AWS Account**.

**Step 2:** Enter your **email address** and an **account name**. Click **Verify email address**. Enter the verification code sent to your email.

**Step 3:** Set the **root user password**.

**Step 4:** Choose the account type: **Personal** (in the video: personal use). Enter your name, address and phone details.

**Step 5:** Enter **card details**. AWS may charge a very small amount (in the video about 2 rupees) only to verify the card. This is normal.

**Step 6:** Complete **identity confirmation**. Enter your phone number and the verification code you receive.

**Step 7:** If it asks for a document type and number, select one you are comfortable with (for example driving licence) and enter it.

**Step 8:** Choose the support plan: **Basic support – Free**. We only use the account for learning and testing.

**Step 9:** Wait for the message that the account is created. You will also receive an email that your account is ready.

**Step 10:** Click **Sign in to the Console** and log in as **Root user** with your email and password.

**Expected result:** the AWS Management Console home page opens.

## Important notes
- The first user you create this way is the **root user**. It has full access to everything. Do NOT use it for daily work. Next chapter creates a safer user.
- Set a **billing alert** or check the Billing dashboard regularly so you do not get surprise charges. (Not shown in the video but recommended.)

## Troubleshooting

### Error 1
```text
Card verification failed / payment method declined
```
**Reason:** International or online payments are disabled on the card, or the card has no balance for the small verification charge.
**Fix:** Enable online/international transactions with your bank, or try another card.

## Final Result
You can log in to the AWS Console as the root user.
